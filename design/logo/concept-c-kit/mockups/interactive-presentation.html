The brief

A distinctive personal identity for Raphael Dacara, focusing on mobile engineering (Flutter, iOS, Android, Full-Stack). It needs to work seamlessly across a portfolio website navbar, app icon, browser favicon, GitHub/LinkedIn avatar, and developer tooling.

engineeredfluidprecisemodernarchitectural

Success: unmistakably mobile without clip-art clichés; reads instantly at 16 px in the browser tab and navbar; scales to high-impact app icons; works in solid monochrome and tech blue.

Concept A

The Viewport Monogram

An engineered smartphone silhouette formed entirely by the interlocking capital initials R and D with an optical dynamic island.

  • Unmistakably mobile: the outer silhouette is a modern smartphone with rounded corners and top notch
  • Dual-chamber monogram: left chamber forms an architectural R, right chamber forms the D and screen viewport
  • Flawless 16 px scale: bold 26-28 px strokes survive extreme reduction to navbar and favicon sizes
  • Passed production audit with 100/100 score and exact 75° geometric angles
